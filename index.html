WHAT IS
DRISHTI-PHARMA?

Drishti-Pharma is an inclusive assistive vision system engineered to eliminate critical medication safety barriers for over 40 million visually impaired citizens and 140 million senior citizens in India. By solving specular glare on curved aluminium blister packaging and applying deterministic calendar validation, it extracts 4pt–6pt micro-print drug names, active formulations, and expiration dates—delivering immediate, life-saving spoken guidance in Indian regional languages without cloud reliance or AI hallucinations.

About Drishti-Pharma

Drishti-Pharma is a specialized assistive platform built to eliminate medication safety barriers for over 40 million visually impaired citizens and 140 million elderly individuals across India.

CORE INNOVATIONS:

• Specular-Invariant Inpainting: Solves foil reflection on curved metallic blister packs using Fast Telea reconstruction and LAB CLAHE equalisation.

• Zero Hallucination Calendar Logic: Enforces deterministic datetime arithmetic rather than guessing faded numbers.

• Multilingual Voice Companion: Clear acoustic announcements in English, Hindi, and Tamil.

Security & Dignity

Healthcare Privacy Policy

Under the National Association for the Blind (NAB) healthcare autonomy principles, an individual's private prescriptions (including psychiatric, pain, or hormone therapy medications) must remain strictly confidential.

✓ Local-First Processing: Image pre-processing and voice synthesis execute entirely within the local browser environment.

✓ Zero Data Retention: No patient photos, prescription records, or biometric data are transmitted to external servers or logged in public databases.

✓ No Third-Party Trackers: The application does not contain advertising cookies or telemetry SDKs.
