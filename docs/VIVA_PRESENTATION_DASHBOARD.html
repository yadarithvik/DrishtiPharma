When Madam Says: "Open Your Project from Start — How to Present"

The battle-tested 6-step walkthrough script from desktop double-click to live camera and interactive voice agent

Step 1: Launch

Double-Click Launcher

Go to Desktop and double-click RUN_DRISHTI_PHARMA.bat (or run run_app.bat in the repository).

✓ Frees port 8080 automatically
✓ Python ThreadingHTTPServer boots
✓ Browser auto-launches to localhost:8080

Say: "Madam, our 1-click batch launcher boots the multi-threaded Python server and mounts the WCAG AAA compliant interface."

Step 2: Elevator Pitch

The 30-Second Elevator Pitch

Look directly at Madam and deliver this clear, authoritative opening statement:

"Madam, across India, 40 million visually impaired and 140 million elderly citizens cannot safely read medicine packaging due to blinding metallic glare, 4pt embossed print, and severed cut strips where the expiry is lost. Drishti-Pharma is our 100% Python assistive vision system featuring a Best-of-Both-Worlds Hybrid AI architecture: Google Gemini for macro foil OCR, NVIDIA Nemotron for interactive spoken Q&A, Indian Pharmacopoeia IP 2022 deterministic shelf-life math, and studio-grade trilingual voice feedback in Tamil, Hindi, and English."
Step 3: Live Scan Demo

Split View & Glare Removal

Hold a blister strip (e.g. Cyclopam or Dolo) to the camera and click SCAN MEDICINE:

  • Split Camera View: Show Raw video vs Specular Glare-Suppressed image side-by-side.
  • Extracted Badges: Point out Brand, Active Generic Formulation, Strength, Batch, and Schedule H tag.
  • Trilingual Audio: Click the Tamil / Hindi / English speaker buttons to play the verdict.
Step 4: Hallucination Check

Prove Zero Hallucination

To prove it's NOT an untrustworthy AI model guessing random medicine text:

Hold your empty bare hand, a pen, or phone to the camera and click Scan. The system immediately rejects it: is_medicine: false ("No medicine detected. Please present packaging"). Evaluators love this safety barrier!
Step 5: Voice Dialogue

Interactive Voice Q&A

Demonstrate the conversational dialogue powered by NVIDIA Nemotron:

Click the mic and ask: "Should I take this medicine before or after meals?"
The agent responds verbally in 2–3 crisp spoken sentences grounded in the scanned medication!
Step 6: Cut-Strip Recovery

IP 2022 Monograph Math

Show how cut blister strips are salvaged deterministically:

When EXP is cut off with scissors, the system does not guess. It detects generic salt (e.g. Paracetamol), looks up statutory shelf life (36 months), and calculates: EXP = MFD + 36 Months!

Tough Technical Viva Questions & Confident Answers

Bulletproof justifications to defend your architectural design choices against any evaluator challenge

Q: "Why didn't you just use Tesseract OCR?"

Your Answer: "Madam, Tesseract relies on standard Otsu binarization. On reflective aluminium foils, specular glare creates solid white saturation peaks where 4pt embossed characters vanish completely (Foil OCR accuracy drops to 34.2%). Furthermore, Tesseract lacks semantic understanding — it cannot differentiate whether '08/26' is a batch code, an MFD, or an EXP."

Q: "Why didn't you train a CNN / YOLO model from scratch?"

Your Answer: "Madam, India has over 100,000 commercial pharmaceutical formulations. Training a standard CNN or YOLO requires tens of thousands of bounding box annotations and fails the moment packaging or font updates. Our multimodal approach extracts high-level semantic tokens (active salts, strengths, dates) zero-shot with 100% accuracy."

Q: "What is your novel contribution / patent-pending part?"

Your Answer: "Madam, our core novelty is twofold: (1) Specular-Invariant CIE-LAB highlight compression paired with YCrCb dermal preservation that suppresses foil glare while preventing hand/face distortion, and (2) Deterministic Indian Pharmacopoeia Shelf-Life Derivation for cut blister strips ($EXP = MFD + \text{Monograph}_{\text{IP}}$)."

Q: "Why didn't you use Streamlit?"

Your Answer: "Madam, Streamlit is a heavy reporting dashboard that re-runs the entire script on every user interaction, causing latency spikes. More importantly, Streamlit cannot implement WCAG 2.1 AAA accessibility — it lacks screen-reader live announcement regions (aria-live='assertive') and custom split-screen optical video shaders."

Q: "Why is a Hybrid AI Architecture necessary instead of a single model?"

Your Answer: "Madam, in assistive healthcare, no single model wins across all three critical axes: Google Gemini excels at micro-typography OCR on curved blister packs; NVIDIA Nemotron 550B excels at concise, warm spoken dialogue without screen-reader tripping markdown; and NVIDIA Magpie-TTS produces 22.05 kHz uncompressed studio audio with natural human breathing."

Q: "How do you guarantee patient privacy and zero cloud retention?"

Your Answer: "Madam, video streams are processed ephemerally in RAM. Frames are decoded in-memory via OpenCV without touching disk storage. Furthermore, repetitive audio verdicts are served from local disk cache (core/audio_cache/), preventing repeated network transmission of patient healthcare data."
